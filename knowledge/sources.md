# Sources and attribution

All card text is an original summary written for this prototype, not copied clinical text.
Every card remains `NEEDS_CLINICAL_REVIEW` until a flight-medicine physician reviews it.

- `nasa_hrp_co2` — NASA Human Research Program, CO₂ and spacecraft environmental health risk summaries. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/
- `nasa_hrp_sleep` — NASA HRP, Sleep Loss, Circadian Desynchronization and Work Overload risk. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risk-of-performance-decrements-and-adverse-health-outcomes-resulting-from-sleep-loss-circadian-desynchronization-and-work-overload/
- `nasa_hrp_immune` — NASA HRP, Immune System Dysregulation risk. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/
- `nasa_hrp_behavioral` — NASA HRP, Behavioral Health and Performance. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/
- `nasa_hrp_neuro` — NASA HRP, Space Motion Sickness / Sensorimotor adaptation. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/
- `nasa_hrp_bone` — NASA HRP, Bone and Muscle Loss. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/
- `nasa_hrp_exercise` — NASA HRP, Exercise Countermeasures. https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/
- `nasa_med_ops` — NASA public materials on medical operations and crew health (exploration medical capability). https://www.nasa.gov/
- `who_fever` — WHO, fever and infection guidance for remote settings. https://www.who.int/
- `who_dehydration` — WHO, oral rehydration salts and dehydration guidance. https://www.who.int/
- `aha_chest_pain` — American Heart Association public education on chest pain and heart attack. https://www.heart.org/
- `google_cmo_da` — Google Public Sector blog, "NASA's CMO-DA" (7 August 2025). https://publicsector.googleblog.com/
